questions

What owners actually ask me.

Will it sound robotic?+

No. It's built on the same voice and text models behind modern phone assistants, tuned to your business and how you actually talk to customers. Most callers don't realize it's not a person until you tell them.

What if it can't answer something?+

It hands off cleanly: takes a message, texts you the details, and tells the caller you'll follow up personally. It never guesses at something it doesn't know, and it's tested to never quote a price it isn't sure of.

Does it work with my calendar or booking tool?+

Yes, in most cases. It connects to Google Calendar, Calendly, Housecall Pro, Jobber, and most common scheduling software. If you use something else, I'll check on the call before we start.

What does setup involve on my end?+

About 30 minutes of your time up front: what to ask callers, what counts as a good lead for you, how you want it to sound. I build it, test it against real conversations, and you approve it before it ever talks to a customer.

Can I turn it off?+

Any time. It's your phone number and your calendar — nothing about that changes. If it's not working for you, we turn it off or fix it. No long lock-in contract.
