A plain-English brief on how your code, credentials, and data are handled. If you need something more formal, an NDA and the data terms in the agreement cover it — tap a topic to expand.

Credentials & access
I use least-privilege access — only what a task needs, and scoped credentials over shared ones. Secrets live in your secret manager or environment, never in code or chat, and access is revoked at handoff.
Confidentiality & NDA
Your project is confidential by default. I'm happy to sign your NDA, or provide a mutual one before we get into specifics. Nothing about your build is shared or shown without your written OK.
Data retention
I keep only what's needed to do the work, for as long as the engagement runs. At handoff, working copies and access are removed from my side — the deliverables live in your repo and infrastructure.
Subprocessors & tools
Any third-party service that would touch your data (a model provider, a CI runner) is named up front, and I'll use your accounts and your chosen providers wherever possible so the data path stays inside your control.
Your code stays yours
You own everything produced. IP assignment is written into the agreement, deliverables ship in your repository, and there's no retained dependency on me to keep it running.

What I always do

  • Least-privilege, scoped access
  • Secrets in your vault, never in code
  • Name every data-touching subprocessor up front
  • Ship to your repo / your CI
  • Revoke access at handoff

What I never do

  • Hardcode or paste your secrets
  • Reuse your data to train anything
  • Show your work without written OK
  • Leave a black box only I can run
  • Retain working copies after handoff

This is a plain-English summary, not the contract. The signed agreement and any NDA are the binding terms.
