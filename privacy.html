Privacy, in plain English.

Effective August 2026 · Sage Ideas LLC

What this site collects

Anonymous usage analytics (page views and button clicks — no cross-site tracking, no ad pixels) and whatever you type into the contact form: name, email, company, and your message. That is the complete list.

What it is used for

Contact form submissions are used to reply to you. Analytics are used to understand which pages earn attention. Nothing is sold, shared, rented, or used for advertising.

Client work

During an engagement, access to your systems is scoped to the engagement, credentials stay in your systems, code stays in your repos, and nothing is retained after handoff. A data-handling one-pager is available before any work starts.

Deletion

Email hello@sageideas.dev and any record of you is deleted within 30 days.
