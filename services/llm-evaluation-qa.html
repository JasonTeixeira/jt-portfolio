questions
Which tools do you use?

Promptfoo and DeepEval where they fit, custom runners where they don't. The tool matters less than the discipline: computed scores from real commands, never opinions.

Our feature uses RAG — does that change the approach?

It adds retrieval-quality evals (context precision/recall, citation coverage) in front of the generation evals. I've shipped RAG systems where 100% of answers carry citations by design.

How long until we have a working gate?

A minimal gate — 30 golden traces and one faithfulness judge in CI — typically lands in the first two weeks. The battery grows from there.
