### 5. Intellectual property

All work product created under this SOW is assigned to Client upon receipt of final payment in full. Until final payment, work product remains the property of Provider. Provider retains ownership of pre-existing tools, templates, and general methods, and grants Client a perpetual license to any that are embedded in the deliverables.

### 6. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only for this engagement. If the parties have signed a separate non-disclosure agreement, that NDA governs and this section supplements it. Provider will not name Client publicly (portfolio, case study, reference) without Client's written permission.

### 7. Data handling and access

- Access is scoped to the engagement: only the systems, repositories, and data required for the deliverables in Section 2.
- Credentials are issued by Client through Client's own systems, and Client may revoke them at any time.
- Code is written in and remains in Client's repositories.
- Upon completion or termination, Provider deletes any Client data in Provider's possession and confirms deletion in writing. Nothing is retained post-engagement.

### 8. Limitation of liability

Provider's total liability under this SOW, from any cause, is capped at the fees actually paid by Client under this SOW. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Deliverables are testing and automation systems; Client remains responsible for its own release decisions.

### 9. Termination

Either party may terminate with {NOTICE_DAYS, e.g., 7} days' written notice. On termination: Client pays for work completed to date; Provider hands over all work product covered by payments received; Section 7 (data deletion) and Section 6 (confidentiality) survive.

### 10. General

Independent contractor relationship; no employment, partnership, or agency is created. This SOW plus any referenced NDA is the entire agreement for this engagement. Governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
