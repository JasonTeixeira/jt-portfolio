Security & data handling

Written for the person who has to sign off on a vendor — honest and specific. No certifications I don't hold, no posture I can't back. Here is exactly how your data and your systems are handled before, during, and after an engagement. If something is a hard gate for you, I'll tell you up front rather than paper over it.

Where your data lives

On your accounts — not mine.

Wherever possible, the work runs on your cloud, your model providers, and your keys. I don't copy your data into my own systems to do the job, and I never train models on it. The deliverable lands in your repository and your CI — so the thing you're paying for lives where your controls already apply.

The ledger · what I always do, and what I never do
Always
  • Least-privilege, scoped access — only what the task needs
  • Secrets in your vault, never in source code
  • Every data-touching subprocessor named up front
  • Ship to your repo / your CI, documented
  • Revoke all access at handoff
Never
  • Train on, resell, or reuse your data
  • Hold production keys longer than the engagement
  • Retain working copies after handoff
  • Introduce a dependency that needs me to keep running
  • Claim a certification or control I don't actually have
Continuity & bus factor

What happens if I'm unavailable?

A fair question for any single operator — so I build so you're never dependent on me. Everything ships into your repo, infrastructure, and keys, documented and tested, with a runbook your team (or the next engineer) can operate without me. The eval harness and the CI gate are the handoff: they keep the system honest whether I'm in the loop or not. Nothing production-critical is gated on my availability, and there's no lock-in — you can part ways at any time and keep running.

The honest answers · the questions a security team actually asks
Certifications

Are you SOC 2 certified?

Honestly: no — I'm a solo operator, not a certified organization, and I won't claim otherwise. What I do is work inside your controls and least-privilege access, sign your security addenda, and meet the requirements your team sets. If a formal certification is a hard gate, I'll say so up front rather than paper over it.

Incident response

What happens if there's a security incident?

I notify you promptly, help contain it, and follow your incident process. Because the work lives in your infrastructure with scoped access, the blast radius is small by design — there's no shadow copy of your data on my side to leak.

Retention & deletion

What do you keep, and for how long?

Only what's needed to do the work, only for as long as the engagement runs. At handoff, working copies and access are removed from my side — the deliverables live in your repo and infrastructure, under your retention policy, not mine.

Liability, E&O & contracts

Paperwork and coverage

Engagements run under a written MSA + SOW with fixed scope and fixed liability terms; an NDA and a DPA are available on request, and I'll work to your data-residency requirements. Professional-liability (E&O) coverage can be put in place, sized to the engagement, when your contract requires it — raise it during scoping and it's handled before kickoff.
