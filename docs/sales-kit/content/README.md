# LinkedIn Content — First 14 Posts

The throughline for every post, verbatim: **"I test and prove AI features for teams shipping LLM products."**

Buyer: founders, eng leaders, and PMs at companies shipping LLM-powered features (chatbots, agents, RAG, generators) whose current test process is "eyeball it."

Each file is ready to paste. Frontmatter (`pillar`, `hook`, `cta_type`) is for your reference — delete it or leave it, LinkedIn ignores it. The `## Why this works` note at the bottom of each post is for you, not the audience — don't paste it.

## Posting cadence

- **3 posts a week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.** These 14 posts cover ~5 weeks (weeks 1–4 full, week 5 Mon + Wed).
- **Best window:** Tue–Thu, 7:30–9:30am your audience's time, tends to get the most reach. Monday and Friday still post — consistency beats perfect timing.
- **One post per day, max.** Don't double up. The algorithm suppresses a second post within ~18 hours of the first.
- **Native text only.** Write the post directly into LinkedIn. Do not attach a link preview in the body — if a post has a link (a repo, a note), put the URL in the **first comment**, not the post, because outbound links in the body cut reach. The posts here that reference a URL are written so the link works fine in a comment.

## Engagement rules (this is half the work)

Posting is maybe 40% of the channel. The other 60% is what you do around the post.

- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Comments in the first 60–90 minutes are the single biggest reach signal. Set a timer, answer each one with a real sentence (not "thanks!"), and ask a follow-up question to keep the thread alive.
- **Comment on 5 target-buyer posts a day.** Find founders, eng leaders, and PMs talking about shipping AI features, and leave a substantive comment — a specific take, not a compliment. This is how the algorithm learns who your audience is and starts showing your posts to people like them. It also puts your name in front of exact-fit buyers before they ever see your posts.
- **Answer DMs like a human.** The free mini-eval offer (W5 Wed) will generate DMs. Reply fast, do the free look, write up the failures plainly. That free diagnostic is the top of the offer ladder — treat it as the most important unpaid work you do.
- **Repurpose what lands.** If a post outperforms, that topic becomes a field note on the site and a short clip. One artifact, four surfaces (see `docs/CONTENT_CADENCE.md`).

## The expectation: 3–9 months to crack the channel

Read this before you post, and re-read it in week 6 when you want to quit.

An owned organic channel is the one path to $10k+/mo that survives adversarial scrutiny — but it is a **compounding** asset, not a slot machine. The verified expectation is **3 to 9 months of consistent publishing before the channel produces reliable inbound.** The first month or two will look like almost nothing: single-digit likes, no DMs, silence. That is not failure. That is every account's baseline before the compounding starts.

What kills people here is quitting at week 6 because the numbers are small, right before the curve turns. The proof you're publishing is genuinely unfakeable — that's your durable edge — but it still needs reps and time to find the audience.

**The commitment that works:** 3 posts a week, every week, plus the daily engagement, for at least 6 months, without judging it by any single week's numbers. Track leading indicators (are you posting on schedule, are you commenting daily, is the follower count drifting up) — not lagging ones (likes, DMs) — until month 3+. Show up on the bad-numbers days. That's the whole game.
