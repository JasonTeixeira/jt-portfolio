Service level agreement · the Operate phase

Ongoing support & maintenance for a live AI system.

When you'd rather a system be run than handed off, this is the shape of the ongoing relationship: what's covered, how fast I respond, how you know it's healthy, and what happens when something drifts. It's a support agreement for a consulting engagement — not a five-nines infrastructure uptime guarantee.

Sample document — not legal advice. This is an illustrative template to show how an Operate engagement is structured. It is not a binding agreement and has not been reviewed for your jurisdiction. Real terms are scoped with you and reviewed by your counsel before signing.
1

Scope of covered services

What this covers. Ongoing operation of the AI system built during the engagement — the feature, its eval/test harness, and the CI gates — running in your infrastructure.

  • Monitoring — health, quality drift, cost, and error-rate signals on the covered system.
  • Maintenance — dependency updates, prompt/model tuning within the agreed scope, and eval-suite upkeep.
  • Regression runs — the eval and test gates continue to run on a defined cadence; failures are triaged.
  • Support — a named channel for issues and questions about the covered system.

Out of scope. New features, net-new integrations, unrelated systems, and incidents caused by third-party outages or changes made by your team outside the covered code. These are quoted separately.

2

Response targets

Targets are for response (acknowledgement and start of work), measured in business hours. Because this is a solo consulting engagement, targets are commitments to responsiveness, not a staffed 24/7 NOC.

SeverityExampleResponse target
S1 · CriticalCovered system down or returning unsafe output to customersSame business day
S2 · HighQuality gate failing, degraded but running1 business day
S3 · NormalNon-urgent question, minor issue, tuning request2–3 business days

Business hours and time zone are stated in the signed agreement. Outside covered hours, S1 issues are handled on a best-effort basis.

3

Reporting & transparency

You get a periodic summary — regression-run results, any drift caught, changes made, and cost. No black box: the monitoring and gates run in your infrastructure, and you can see them any time.

4

Commercials & term

Retainer. A monthly retainer sized to the covered scope and expected activity, agreed in writing. indicative, scoped per engagement

Term. Month-to-month unless a fixed term is agreed, with a notice period for either party. Nothing is retained on my side after the relationship ends — the system continues to run in your infrastructure.
