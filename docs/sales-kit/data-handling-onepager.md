# How I Handle Your Data

**Sage Ideas LLC · Jason Teixeira · Orlando, FL**

Short version: I touch as little as possible, through your systems, and keep nothing when I'm done.

## Access is scoped to the engagement

I get access only to the systems, repos, and data the written scope requires. Nothing else. If the work doesn't need production data, I don't ask for it.

## Credentials come from your systems

You issue access through your own tools — your identity provider, your repo permissions, your VPN. You can see everything I can see, and you can revoke it in one click, any time. I never ask you to share passwords or move credentials through email or chat.

## NDA-friendly

I'll sign your NDA before seeing anything sensitive, or provide mine if you don't have one. Either works. I don't name clients publicly without written permission.

## Nothing retained after handoff

When the engagement ends, any client data in my possession is deleted, and I confirm that in writing. No copies, no "just in case" backups on my side.

## No subprocessors by default

By default, no third party touches your data through me. If a specific tool in scope would send data to a third-party service, I name it in the SOW before work starts and you approve it first.

## Code stays in your repos

Everything I build is committed to your repositories from day one. There is no separate codebase to hand over and no dependency on my infrastructure after I leave.
