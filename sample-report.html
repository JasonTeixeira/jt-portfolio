What I'd gate before release

  • Injection + jailbreak boundary. The assistant obeys injected instructions. A grounding-and-refusal layer plus an eval that fails the build on any injection bypass.
  • A grounding gate for facts. It invents refund and upgrade policies. Bind answers to a source-of-truth and fail the eval when it states unverifiable specifics.
  • A scope guard. It leaves its lane on request. A cheap classifier + refusal keeps it on support.
